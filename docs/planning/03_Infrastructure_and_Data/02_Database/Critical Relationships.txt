MVP status: `DATABASE_SCHEMA.md` is canonical. A conversation has one optional
project association, which is its only persisted active project; it does not
have a distinct project-state relation. Topics/tasks are conversation-scoped,
and entity registry/reference resolution supplies named-entity lineage.
