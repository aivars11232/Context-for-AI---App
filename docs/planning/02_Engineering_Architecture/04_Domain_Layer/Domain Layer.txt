MVP status: canonical domain types and policy behavior are in
`docs/contracts/DomainAndDecisionRules.md`. The names below are historical
examples. Do not implement `User` (the MVP is single-operator), a duplicate
`ProjectState`, or duplicated policy packages. `ConversationState`, topics,
tasks, entities, memory revisions, processing runs, and typed outcomes are the
canonical MVP model.
