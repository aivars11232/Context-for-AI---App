When validation fails, the app should not immediately show the bad answer.
Instead:
AI response
    ↓
Validator detects failure
    ↓
Correction instructions generated
    ↓
AI revises response
    ↓
Validator checks again
    ↓
Approved response shown

Set a strict retry limit, such as:
Maximum automatic corrections: 2

After that, persist and return a controlled failure rather than showing the
known-invalid result or allowing an endless loop.
